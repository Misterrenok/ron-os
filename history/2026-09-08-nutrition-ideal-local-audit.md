## Material unresolved nutrition items
1. **Manganese is newly reopened as an audit item.** EFSA could not establish a manganese UL and instead set a conservative safe level of 8 mg/day for adults from all sources. A screening estimate using generic USDA-like donors for the current ordinary-day oats (80 g), whole-wheat bread (100 g), walnuts (15 g), cooked lentils (100 g), almonds (30 g) and white wheat bread (170 g) totals roughly **8.85 mg/day before pasta/vegetables/water**. This is NOT product-specific truth: Nimet/Uno/İpek product manganese is unreported and donor transfer may materially over/underestimate. Therefore do not diagnose toxicity or silently change the menu, but do not claim 95+ ideality until manganese is reconciled with best available product/category evidence. Exceeding EFSA's conservative safe level is not itself proof of harm.
2. **Vitamin D** remains not reliably food-covered. Adult reference is 15 mcg / 600 IU/day. The previously proposed 600-IU strategy remains a proposal, not actual intake or diagnosed deficiency.
3. **Iodine** remains numerically uncertain. Adult target is ~150 mcg/day; milk/yogurt/eggs/fish are plausible sources, but Turkish product iodine varies and is not reliably on labels. Do not add 150 mcg blindly just because Cronometer fields are incomplete.
4. **Rotation/diversity** is good enough for launch but repetitive for an aspirational long-term pattern: two main fruits, one main legume and a narrow vegetable set. After launch/tolerance is proven, rotate seasonal fruit, vegetable colours, legumes and oily-fish species without increasing food volume or recipe burden. No magic “30 plants” target is required.
5. **Refined-grain share** (white bread/Etimek/pasta) is a mild long-horizon weakness, not an acute problem because fibre, activity and minimally processed foods are strong. Phase-2 partial swaps to bulgur/potato/whole-grain pasta/other legumes are optional only if calorie adherence and GI comfort remain good.
6. **Actual taste/GI/cold-chain/adherence** remain execution unknowns; ~42 g fibre/day may be excellent if tolerated but should be reduced/redistributed if repeated bloating/fullness causes incomplete eating.
