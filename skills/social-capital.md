# Skill — social capital

Scope: build and maintain a high-quality professional/opportunity network for learning, collaboration, mentorship, information flow and access to opportunities. This is not a general relationship-management system and must not instrumentalize ordinary family, friendship or romantic relationships.

## Runtime route
- Read `domains/social-capital.md` first for current network-system state.
- Add XMind/context for strategic direction and ordinary relationship boundaries; add schedule/finance/learning/e-commerce/mobility only when they materially affect the network objective.
- Contact identity/details are not GitHub-owned current truth. Use direct Ron evidence or an appropriate live contact source only when the task actually requires a specific person.
- Do not infer relationship strength, trust or willingness to help from a saved contact, follower count, employer, old message or shared group.

## Social-capital loop
1. **Objective:** recover the current strategic horizon and why a network would help; no networking for its own sake.
2. **Target circles:** identify the few communities/roles that could materially improve information, learning, collaboration or opportunity flow. Prefer diversity across relevant circles over raw contact count.
3. **Gap map:** distinguish existing trusted relationships, weak ties, missing roles/communities and unknowns. Keep unverified relationship quality `UNKNOWN`.
4. **Value-first contact:** create a legitimate reason to interact—useful information, concrete help, shared work, thoughtful question, introduction or contribution—without pretending intimacy or using manipulative scripts.
5. **Reciprocity:** make requests specific and low-burden; help when reasonable; do not treat people as transactions or maintain a hidden debt ledger.
6. **Maintenance:** follow up when there is real context, shared progress or a useful touchpoint. Avoid spammy cadence and quota-chasing.
7. **Bridge:** when appropriate, deepen a useful weak tie into repeated reciprocal interaction, collaboration, mentorship or mutual introduction.
8. **Measure quality:** prioritize reciprocal relationships, response/help exchanges, collaborations, introductions, trusted information flow and diversity of circles. Followers and total contacts are weak proxies.
9. **Review:** periodically assess which circles are strategically useful, neglected, overconcentrated or no longer relevant; prune process, not people.

## Boundaries
- Family/friendship/romantic life remains ordinary relationship territory unless Ron explicitly asks about a professional/opportunity aspect.
- Never fake friendship, status, scarcity, urgency, affection or reciprocity to extract value.
- Never mass-message, scrape, publish, or contact anyone without exact task authority.
- Persist only minimal non-sensitive continuity in GitHub. Avoid personal contact details, private conversation content or unnecessary names; store role/context/status when sufficient.
- No Gmail, Contacts, Calendar, social-platform or messaging mutation is authorized merely because this skill recommends outreach.

This skill owns procedure only, never the live contact graph or private correspondence.
