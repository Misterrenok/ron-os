# Financial Independence Master Factor Map

Status: **STABLE REFERENCE / NOT A MUTABLE STATE OWNER**
Created: **2026-09-28 Europe/Istanbul**

Purpose: preserve the agreed near-comprehensive causal map of controllable factors that can increase the probability, speed, resilience or option value of reaching financial independence (FI).

## Interpretation
- This is a causal checklist/reference, not a claim that every item has an independently measured effect size.
- Tier order is approximate. Adjacent item numbers are not a scientifically precise universal ranking.
- FI is a working objective serving autonomy, not a terminal value that automatically overrides health, safety, legality, close relationships, meaning or other durable human goods.
- Current personal state never lives here. Dated assessments belong in history; mutable truth remains with the exact Ron OS/live owner.
- Re-rank only when stronger evidence shows a material causal omission or ordering error. Do not grow the list for cosmetic completeness.

## Score convention for dated assessments
5 = strongly covered / proven  
4 = good  
3 = partial / adequate  
2 = weak  
1 = major gap  
0 = confirmed active drag  
? = insufficient current evidence

## External / weakly controllable context
Keep in the model but do not confuse with controllable actions: genetics, innate traits, country/citizenship at birth, family resources, starting capital, childhood health/education, inheritance, wars, political crises, recessions, inflation shocks, technological regime shifts, random meetings, random manager quality, cohort/timing effects, market returns during accumulation, sequence-of-returns near FI, unexpected illness/accident and luck.

## Compression
The 240 controllable factors collapse into ten mechanisms:
1. Do not break the human engine.
2. Become highly valuable to an expensive market.
3. Gain access to that market legally/geographically.
4. Grow income.
5. Keep a large investable surplus.
6. Convert surplus into ownership/productive capital.
7. Let capital compound for a long time.
8. Avoid ruin.
9. Increase the surface area for good opportunities.
10. Repeat, measure and switch when evidence changes.
