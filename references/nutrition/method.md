# Nutrition analysis method

Updated: 2026-08-21
Scope: durable reasoning/evidence method only. Current menu/state belongs to `domains/nutrition.md`.

## Data-quality hierarchy

1. Physical product label / manufacturer for reported product-specific fields.
2. High-quality complete composition database donor for unreported fields when category transfer is reasonable.
3. Peer-reviewed or laboratory evidence when the transfer assumption is explicit and decision-relevant.
4. Missing data stay UNKNOWN; never infer physiological zero from a blank database field.

## Analysis dimensions

When decision-relevant, evaluate energy balance, protein amount/distribution/quality, essential fatty acids, vitamins/minerals, fiber/food variety, food matrix and bioavailability, relevant nutrient interactions, preparation/storage, contaminants, hydration, adherence/logistics, price/availability and actual outcome trends. Do not maximize every biomarker/nutrient independently at the expense of the system.
