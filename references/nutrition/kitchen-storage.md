# Kitchen and storage method

Updated: 2026-08-21
Scope: durable preparation/storage rules only. Current quantities, days and menu belong to `domains/nutrition.md` and live tasks.

- Perishable prepared food should be cooled/stored promptly and transported with an adequate cold chain when it will spend material time outside refrigeration.
- At work, use the available refrigerator when it is actually available; do not infer current workplace equipment from an old snapshot if Ron reports a change.
- Bulk-prep quantities must be derived from the current approved week plan after any retro/change; do not hardcode an old weekly egg or meal count here.
- Preparation changes that alter energy, water, edible yield or nutrient accounting must be reflected in the model when material.
- Current operational prep times and shopping routes belong in live TickTick/Calendar, not this reference.
