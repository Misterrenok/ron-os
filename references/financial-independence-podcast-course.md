## NotebookLM master instruction
Create a Russian-language educational audio course about financial independence using the uploaded FI factor map and baseline as primary sources.

The course must teach the listener to understand and apply every FI001–FI240 factor, not merely summarize the list.

Prioritize causal importance in this approximate order:
S > A+ > A > A− > B+ > B > B− > C+ > C > C− > D.

Within a tier, spend more teaching time on scores 0–1, then 2, then important ?, then 3, and least on already-strong 4–5. Never invent the user's state where the baseline says ?.

For each important factor, explain naturally:
- what it means in plain Russian;
- why and how it affects FI;
- whether the causal link is direct or indirect;
- a good and bad example;
- common reasoning errors;
- practical ways to improve it;
- how to measure real improvement;
- interactions with other factors;
- when further optimization stops being worth it.

Teach rather than motivate. Define technical terms. Use numerical examples, counterexamples, causal chains, and short "what would you choose and why?" pauses. Distinguish correlation from causation, facts from hypotheses, expected value from guaranteed outcomes, and reversible from irreversible decisions.

Do not teach "money at any cost". FI serves autonomy and must be evaluated alongside health, safety, legality, close relationships, time, downside risk and future option value.

Passive exposure is not mastery. At the end of each episode include:
- 5–10 key takeaways;
- 3–5 recall/self-test questions;
- 1–3 practical experiments where useful;
- exact FI IDs covered;
- the next logical block.

The course is complete only after every FI001–FI240 has been substantively covered at least once.

4. FI067–FI090 — communication, sales, negotiation, career packaging, reputation and network.

6. FI116–FI137 — business, market validation, unit economics, distribution, systems and equity.
7. FI138–FI158 — environment design, habits, checklists, reviews, decision systems and automation.
8. FI159–FI174 — partner, relationships, peers, status behavior, cooperation and conflict.
9. FI175–FI194 — insurance, cybersecurity, contracts, concentration risk and contingency plans.
10. FI195–FI210 — curiosity, experiments, side projects, opportunity surface and option value.
11. FI211–FI240 — commute, environment, energy hygiene, routines, professional presentation, social micro-skills and recovery.
12. Final synthesis — connect all 240 factors into one causal model and explain bottleneck-first improvement.

Longer multi-part treatment is preferred over superficial compression when NotebookLM supports it.
