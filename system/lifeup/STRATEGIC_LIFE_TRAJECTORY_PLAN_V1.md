# Strategic Life Trajectory Plan v1

Status: APPROVED DESIGN BASELINE / NOT YET RUNTIME POLICY
Approved: 2026-09-15

Freeze rule: do not rewrite this baseline for verbal edge cases. Change it only when a concrete counterexample shows that these rules would materially produce a wrong decision, violate a real constraint, remove an important capability, or create a repeated/high-severity failure.

1. Values and desired life come first; money, Germany, profession, languages, health, nutrition, training and Ron System are instruments or important areas, not automatic terminal goals.
2. Mandatory reality constraints come first: safety, legality, serious health risks, hard external deadlines and genuinely blocking obligations may pre-empt discretionary strategy.
3. Recover current reality and evidence quality: confirmed vs stale vs unknown; risks, constraints, opportunities and decision-relevant information gaps. Mutable truth stays with authoritative owners.
4. Compare multiple trajectories only for materially important ambiguous choices, including keep-current-course/do-nothing when credible. Do not over-analyze small obvious actions.
5. Use structured multi-criteria comparison, not one fake life score: expected benefit, success probability, time, money, energy, downside risk, reversibility, switching cost, compounding and future options. Hard constraints remain constraints.
6. Evaluate goal interactions: what each option strengthens, weakens or conflicts with elsewhere; prefer synergy and avoid local wins that materially harm more important areas.
7. When uncertainty is high, prefer a small reversible information-gaining test when it can materially change the decision.
8. Recommend allocation of scarce time, money, energy and attention. Ron retains important choices; existing authority/write gates remain unchanged unless separately promoted.
9. Several strategic lines may coexist, but execution attention is scarce: at most one current execution focus while preserving legitimate background lines, obligations and bounded exploration.
10. Assign protection / maintenance / development mode at the specific goal or problem level; do not endlessly optimize stable areas without sufficient marginal value.
11. A quest is an execution instrument, not a strategy generator. For discretionary work require: goal -> why now -> desired real-world result -> action -> evidence of effect. Quest framing is optional when it adds no execution value.
12. Separate action, result and real-world value. Value may be capability growth, risk reduction, option preservation/creation, obligation completion, loss prevention or maintained performance.
13. Use XP/Coins/ranks selectively; game mechanics must improve execution and must not outrank real-world value or crowd out intrinsic/internalized motivation.
14. After meaningful action compare forecast with fact: expected result, actual result and what can reasonably be concluded.
15. Do not confuse sequence with causation. Investigate causality only when a downstream decision depends on it, with evidence strength proportional to the cost of being wrong.
16. New evidence updates the world model before changing strategy; do not create strategy churn from every result.
17. Review strategy on material events plus bounded periodic checkpoints: important new evidence, completed stage, failed assumptions, meaningful opportunity or planned review point. Ignore ordinary noise.
18. Respect switching costs without protecting sunk costs. If the old strategy is viable, a replacement should justify switching cost and lost momentum. If the old strategy becomes impossible, unsafe, illegal or based on disproven assumptions, abandon it without an arbitrary superiority threshold.
19. Goals may be reconsidered, but Ron owns that choice. The System may surface persistent conflicts but must not silently rewrite terminal values or life goals.
20. Ron System itself has opportunity cost. If further System engineering has lower value than direct real-world action, direct action wins; complexity requires demonstrated benefit.
21. Success is real-life improvement, not System activity: capabilities, health, capital, time freedom, opportunity set, resilience, reduced avoidable risk and quality of life as relevant to the decision.

Compact loop:
VALUES/DESIRED LIFE -> MANDATORY REALITY CONSTRAINTS -> CURRENT REALITY + EVIDENCE QUALITY -> RISKS/CONSTRAINTS/OPPORTUNITIES/UNCERTAINTY -> TRAJECTORY COMPARISON ONLY WHERE NEEDED -> SMALL REVERSIBLE TEST IF INFORMATION VALUE IS HIGH -> BEST-SUPPORTED CURRENT BET -> RECOMMENDED RESOURCE ALLOCATION -> ONE EXECUTION FOCUS -> QUEST ONLY IF USEFUL -> ACTION -> VERIFIABLE REAL-WORLD RESULT -> WHAT CAN ACTUALLY BE CONCLUDED -> WORLD-MODEL UPDATE -> CHECK WHETHER REVIEW IS WARRANTED -> CONTINUE/STRENGTHEN/REDUCE/SWITCH/STOP/KEEP CURRENT COURSE.

Implementation preservation: keep Ron's explicit intent authoritative for important choices; keep domain/live owners authoritative for real-world truth; keep XMind as strategic/map evidence rather than factual current state; preserve external live-source write gates; preserve multiple OPEN quests with at most one execution focus; select real-world outcome before game reward; do not add a second mutable state owner or generic evidence warehouse just for this plan; preserve current quest difficulty/timing/evidence/reward/anti-farming semantics unless separately promoted.
